# IMD Radar hackathon entry (the form allows 20–1,000 characters)

LastSeenIMD is a free tool for people whose crypto wallet was drained. Paste any address: it checks 28 EVM chains and Solana, finds stolen keys (sweepers, EIP-7702 delegations), drains and address poisoning, traces where the money went until an exchange, and explains it all in plain words, in a Fears to Fathom-style horror game look. GLM 5.3 writes the analysis, SubQ gives a second opinion, and posts about the address on X are surfaced.

The IMD swarm is the judge. LastSeenRegistry, launched through IMD on Robinhood Chain, turns a case into a yes/no question the swarm answers from chain data. Only a signed verdict that 4 of 5 agents agree on adds the attacker to a public do-not-send list any wallet can read for free. Confirmed cases carry IMD bounties: hunters trace the money hop by hop, and the swarm verifies every hop before paying.
