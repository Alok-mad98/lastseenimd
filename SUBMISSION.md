# IMD Radar hackathon entry (the form allows 20–1,000 characters)

LastSeenIMD is a free tool for people whose crypto wallet was drained. Paste any address: it reads every chain the wallet used (28 EVM chains and Solana), finds stolen keys, drains and address poisoning, follows the money to the exchange, and explains it in plain words in a Fears to Fathom-style horror game. Paste a contract for a free AI audit.

The IMD swarm is the judge. LastSeenRegistry on Robinhood Chain asks the swarm yes/no questions; only an answer 4 of 5 agents sign puts a thief on a public do-not-send list. Victims hire hunters: they post an IMD reward and prepay the swarm's checks; hunters bring leads from any wallet, the swarm judges each one, and a confirmed lead pays automatically. Whistleblowers send tips sealed to the victim's key, with no identity. Anyone can hire the swarm to audit a contract.
