# The launch film

42 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, made entirely from code. Output: [`../docs/launch.mp4`](../docs/launch.mp4).

| Step | Command | Makes |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1. 3D shots | `blender -b -P blender/scene.py` | `blender/frames/shotA` (road, 90 frames) and `shotB` (the man under the lamp, 75 frames), 480×270 at 15 fps |
| 2. Encode | `ffmpeg -framerate 15 -start_number 1 -i blender/frames/shotA/f_%04d.png -vf "scale=1920:1080:flags=neighbor,fps=30,format=yuv420p" hyperframes/assets/shotA.mp4` (same for shotB from frame 101) | the shots, upscaled with hard pixels |
| 3. Logo | `python logo/make_logo.py` | `logo.png`, `logo-icon.png` (copy both and `VT323-Regular.ttf` into `hyperframes/assets/`) |
| 4. Sound | `python audio/build_audio.py <path to ffmpeg>` | `hyperframes/assets/mix.wav` and `audio/cues.json` (every sound effect, with the frame it lands on) |
| 5. Render | `cd hyperframes && npx hyperframes lint && npx hyperframes render --output ../../docs/launch.mp4` | the film |

`shots.cjs` takes the README screenshots of the live app with headless Chrome.

The sound effects are CC0 recordings listed in [`../public/sfx/CREDITS.md`](../public/sfx/CREDITS.md). The music is generated by `build_audio.py`.
